Write a python program that does the reverse process of a spectrogram: convert an image to a mono sound.
Take lossless png as input and treat the background color as silence.
Scan the image from left to right.
Every pixel in a row creates a sound of a frequency that is determined by the y coordinate of the pixel and a duration that is fixed by simply the total duration divided by the width of the image.
The tone that every pixel generates is a sinus curve.
Two or more pixels in a horizontal line should create a continuous sound, i.e. you have to take the phase of the sinus curve into account.
The sounds of all pixels in one row should be simply added.

input parameters are:
a) the minimum frequency (for pixels at the bottom line of the image)
b) the maximum frequency (for pixels at the top line of the image)
c) total duration of the sound

Choose an appropiate format for the output sound.
